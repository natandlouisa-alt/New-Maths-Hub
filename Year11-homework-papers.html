Year 11 Homework Policy — Half Past Papers

A consistent, low-workload weekly homework cycle for Year 11

Purpose

A consistent, low-workload weekly homework cycle for Year 11, using Hannah Kettle Maths' "Half Past Papers" — GCSE past papers split into odd/even question halves (~45 minutes each), self-marked using video walkthroughs.

The Resource

  • Source: hannahkettlemaths.co.uk/half-past-papers
  • Format: each week releases one Foundation and one Higher half-paper (odds one week, evens the next, from the same full past paper), each with a QR code linking to a YouTube walkthrough.
  • Live support: Hannah runs each paper live on YouTube/TikTok every Thursday evening (Foundation 7–7:30pm, Higher 7:30–8pm), which students can watch live, catch up on afterwards, or use as a revision resource any time.
  • Papers are numbered sequentially (Paper 1, 2, 3…) and issued weekly on a rolling schedule — teachers should set the paper matching the current week number and tier.

Weekly Cycle

DayAction
ThursdayNew paper set as homework (correct tier: Foundation or Higher)
Following ThursdayHomework due — student brings in completed and self-marked paper
Start point: the cycle begins in Week 2 of the schedule (not Week 1), so students set off one paper behind the live release — this gives them a paper that already has a walkthrough video available in good time, rather than racing the live stream.

Runs continuously, week on week, for the rest of the year.

Student Expectations

  1. Complete the paper independently — allow 30–45 minutes.
  2. Mark it themselves against the answers/walkthrough, allowing extra time on top of the 30–45 minutes for marking.
  3. Use the QR code video either:
    • the evening it's set (can watch live or as a replay), to help while completing, or
    • as catch-up support afterwards, if they got stuck or want to check their marking.
  4. Bring the completed, self-marked paper to the following Thursday's lesson.

Quality Assurance

QA happens in lesson, during in-class exam practice — while students are working on their own exam-style questions, the teacher circulates and pulls out Half Past Papers from the previous week to check:

  • it's been attempted in full
  • it's been marked (not just attempted)
  • marking looks honest/accurate against the answers

This should happen most weeks on a sampling basis (not necessarily every paper, every week) so it doesn't require separate lesson time.

Any student without a completed/marked paper is flagged per the department's normal homework follow-up process.

Staff Actions Checklist

  • Set the correct week's paper (right tier) every Thursday
  • Remind students papers are self-marked, video-supported
  • Build in a QA sweep during in-class exam practice each week
  • Follow up incomplete/unmarked papers via standard homework procedure
